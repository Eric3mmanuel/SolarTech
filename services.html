Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

How do I know what size solar system I need?

A load assessment is the best starting point. We consider appliance wattage, usage hours, backup duration, site conditions and your budget before recommending a system.

Do your prices include installation?

Product guide prices generally refer to equipment only unless explicitly stated. Installation, transport, accessories and commissioning are quoted according to the project scope.

Can SolarTech maintain an existing system?

Yes. Share your system details and location so we can assess the equipment and recommend an inspection or maintenance visit.

Do you serve clients outside Nairobi?

We aim to support projects across Kenya. Travel and mobilisation costs may apply depending on the location and project size.
