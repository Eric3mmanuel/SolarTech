Who we are

Building a brighter, energy-smart future.

SolarTech is a Kenyan solar technology brand focused on making dependable renewable energy solutions easier to understand, access and maintain.

Our story

Technology with a purpose.

SolarTech was established around a simple idea: solar energy should be practical, well-designed and supported by people who care about the outcome.

We bring together solar products, technical services and customer support to serve households, enterprises, farms and institutions. Our approach begins with understanding the customer's needs rather than simply selling equipment.

As the company grows, our ambition is to contribute to Kenya's clean-energy transition through responsible solutions, technical knowledge and lasting customer relationships.
