# SolarTech Kenya — Company Website

A responsive, six-page static website starter for SolarTech.

## Pages
- `index.html` — Home
- `products.html` — Product catalogue, filters and indicative price guide
- `services.html` — Services, service rates and FAQs
- `about.html` — Company overview, vision, mission and values
- `projects.html` — Project types and applications (not represented as completed projects)
- `contact.html` — Contact details and quote enquiry form

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this folder with a local static web server.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `+254 7XX XXX XXX` and the placeholder `tel:` number with your official number.
2. Confirm that `info@solartech.co.ke` is created and configured.
3. Add your official postal/physical address, social accounts and business hours.
4. Confirm all product and service prices against supplier quotations, specifications, tax treatment and installation scope.
5. Replace stock photography with SolarTech's own project photos as they become available.
6. Set up a real form backend or CRM if you want enquiries stored automatically. The current form opens the visitor's email application and does not transmit data to a server.
7. Check applicable Kenyan licensing, electrical safety and regulatory requirements before advertising or undertaking regulated installations.

## Design
- Brand palette: navy, solar yellow, renewable green and white.
- Fonts: Montserrat (headings), Poppins (navigation / labels), Inter (body).
- Images are loaded from Unsplash over the internet; internet access is required for those images and Google Fonts.


## Visual refresh
- The supplied SolarTech logo is used in the navigation and footer (`assets/solartech-logo.webp`).
- Added scroll-triggered reveal animations, a reading progress bar, back-to-top control, refined hover states and responsive navigation behaviour.
- Respects the visitor's reduced-motion accessibility preference.
- Enquiry form validates in the browser and opens the visitor's email application; it does not store or submit information to a server.
